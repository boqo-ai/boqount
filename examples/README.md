# Examples

`quillfern/` is what a person's folder looks like after boqount's onboarding and its first research, for an
invented brand: Quillfern, a plant-care app that reminds you when to water (`@quillfern`, `quillfern.example`).

Everything in it is invented. Quillfern does not exist, and neither do its handles, its numbers, its survey or the
studies its strategy cites; every web address ends in `.example`, a name reserved so that it can never be a real site.
The facts point at `quillfern/README.md`, which stands in for the project the onboarding read.

What is here:

- `marketing/config.md`: two channels, Instagram and TikTok, both published by hand; calibration; sign-ups typed in
  by hand; no git commits.
- `marketing/queue.md`: the first week, two episodes of the account's series and one post about the product.
- `marketing/strategy.md`: the research's strategy, with its benchmarks, its KPI standards and the review date.
- `marketing/signups.csv`: three weeks of sign-ups typed in before the first post.
- `brand/quillfern/`: the brand book, the accounts, the assets, the logo and the style.

A real folder also holds `marketing/state.md`, `marketing/log.md`, the onboarding notes and the research edition the
sources name, and `films/` once the film project is set up. They are left out here to keep the example short.

`tests/example.test.mjs` runs the skill's tools on this folder, so it stays in the shape the tools read.
