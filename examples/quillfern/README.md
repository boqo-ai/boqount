# Quillfern

> Invented for the boqount example (`../README.md`). No such app, company or site exists.

Quillfern remembers when each of your plants needs water, so you don't have to.

## Who it is for

People with 5 to 30 houseplants who have lost one to a watering they forgot.

## What it does

- You add a plant by its name or a photo, and Quillfern knows what it drinks.
- It sends one reminder on the day a plant needs water, and none on the days it does not.
- Tap Done and the count starts again.
- The day moves with the season: in winter most plants drink less.

## Plans

- Free: up to 5 plants.
- Plus: unlimited plants and a shared household, $3.99 a month or $29 a year.

## How we talk

- "Water day", never "task" or "chore".
- Quillfern reminds you; you do the watering. We never promise that a plant will live.
- Plain words, short sentences, a little dry humour about the plants, never about the person.
