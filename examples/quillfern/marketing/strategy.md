# Quillfern content strategy

Edition: 2026-11-10 (`launch`). Before it: none. Report: `marketing/research/2026-11-10/report.md`;
the table of contents is `marketing/research.md`. Brand facts come from `brand/quillfern/` (v1, approved 2026-11-11),
never from this file.

Every item carries its evidence line: a URL with a short quote and the date read, or our own data with its file and
date. An item with no evidence is not written. An item says which channel it is about when it differs by channel.
The sections below are these ten, in this order.

Everything here is invented for the boqount example: the `*.example` sites do not exist, and the research notes
named below are the ones a real edition would write.

## The account's goal

Be the account people with a shelf of houseplants would follow if Quillfern were not in it, so Quillfern is the name
they hold when a plant dries out. For this brand that means the plants' side of things: why a pot drowns, what a
radiator does to a fern, what the light does in December, said plainly and with a little dry humour.
No organic post asks for anything; the call lives on the profile (`brand/quillfern/brand-book.md` → CTA). A post is
judged on its type's main metric against its norm. Sign-ups are read for a period, three ways, never per post
(Metrics and norms → Sign-ups). The direction is reviewed on 2027-01-11 (Metrics and norms → Review).
- Evidence: brand-book → Positioning (2026-11-09); the person: "teach people about their plants, sell the app on
  the side" (2026-11-11); our data: no posts yet, `marketing/signups.csv` read 2026-11-10.

## Content pillars by type

One pillar per type: what a post of that type is about, for whom, in its layer (`<skill>/hooks.md` → The arc). The
layer suggests the type; the human picks the type at the gate.
- **reach** (`niche`): the plant's side of the windowsill, in the three series in Rubrics. Evidence:
  `notes-formats.md` §2, plant explainers held viewers longer than plant tours (https://studies.example/plant-video,
  "explainers: 41% watched past 3 s", read 2026-11-10).
- **awareness** (`product`): the forgotten watering, with the reminder in the middle. Evidence: `README.md:9`
  (read 2026-11-09).
- **trust** (`product`): the reminder's honest arithmetic: one message on the right day, none on the others.
  Evidence: `README.md:14` (read 2026-11-09).
- **sale** (`offer`): none until a real offer has a real date; brand-book → Facts lists none. Evidence:
  https://quillfern.example/pricing, "no offers right now", read 2026-11-09.

## Metrics and norms

Main metrics: reach `views_72h`; awareness `follows_per_1000_views_7d`; trust `comments_7d`; sale `signups_7d`. Read
by `node <skill>/tools/account-state.mjs norms --channel <channel>` (run it for each channel in `channels`): one row
per type, `type | metric | value | source | date`, in its main metric only. One row per type and no channel: the row
holds the value for Instagram, the account's first channel, and it is the starting norm on TikTok too until TikTok
has five posts of its own type.

```benchmarks
reach | views_72h | 640 | notes-platform.md §4: median views at 72 h of 30 plant-care Reels from Instagram accounts under 5000 followers (studies.example/plant-video; invented) | 2026-10-31
awareness | follows_per_1000_views_7d | 3.1 | notes-platform.md §4: median follows per 1000 views at 7 d of the same 30 Reels (studies.example/plant-video; invented) | 2026-10-31
trust | comments_7d | 6 | notes-platform.md §5: median comments at 7 d on 18 posts that 60% or more followers saw (studies.example/plant-video; invented) | 2026-10-31
```

The research sets no sale benchmark (`signups_7d`); sale's norm waits for 5 own posts.

**KPI standards** (`<skill>/kpi.md`), read by `node <skill>/tools/account-state.mjs kpi --channel <channel>`.

each comes from accounts that post four times a week, so on fewer posts it is a ceiling more than a median.

### Sign-ups

- **The profile link:** https://quillfern.example/start on both channels, no tag; its sign-ups stand in the
  untagged total.
- **Campaigns:** none.
- **"How did you hear about us?":** the person reads the answers from the app's sign-up form each Monday.

Per month: the profile link's sign-ups and the answers. An organic `sale` post has no sign-up figure of its own.

### Review

- **Date:** 2027-01-11, 61 days from the person's approval of the direction.
- **Compared:** the per-film medians of views at 72 hours of the posts from 2026-11-16, against the reach benchmark
  above (there are no posts before them); sign-ups a week in the review's last 28 days against the three weeks in
  `marketing/signups.csv` before 2026-11-09 (14, 11 and 17).
- **Stop rule:** if the median of views at 72 hours is below 320 (half the benchmark) and sign-ups a week are lower
  than 14, the direction has failed on the account's own data.
- **Limits, known now:** about 16 posts on each channel by then; the holidays fall inside the period; the sign-ups
  baseline is three weeks.
- Evidence: the person: "yes to the 11th of January and to that rule" (2026-11-11); the baseline:
  `marketing/signups.csv`, read 2026-11-10.

## What not to do

- No fake engagement in any form: no mutual likes, pods, view farms or bought metrics (cite the platform's rule and the law that applies, for example FTC 16 CFR 465 in the US).
- No false claim. No realistic AI without its label. No other person's face, name or channel without their written
  permission, and no leaked or pre-release material.
- No call in an organic post: nothing in the voice, on the picture or in the caption asks for a comment, a code
  word, a follow, a click or a visit, and no post ends on a logo or a link. The one exception is a post the human
  will promote with bought views, on their word for that post: it carries only the brand book's call line
  (brand-book → CTA).
- No product in a `niche` film: not in a line, on a frame or in the caption.
- No per-post link: a post's Link is the profile link, shown in no post; the ledger's `code_word` and `utm_campaign`
  stay empty, except on an ad post.
- No material that is not the brand's without the human's word for that source (brand-book → Do and don't).
- No batch posting to catch up on missed days.
- No sale post while the funnel is broken.
- No spend over the budget. No autonomous act missing from the report.
- No post goes out by a route the config's `publish_via` does not allow, "because it is faster".
- A post is a video file: no carousel posts.
- No promise that a plant will live: Quillfern reminds, the person waters (`README.md:26`, read 2026-11-09).
- No dead or rotting plant as a joke: the person's loss is never the punchline (the person, 2026-11-11).
- No "plant mom" or "plant parent" talk: in `notes-competitors.md` §1 it was the most worn phrase of the niche (read
  2026-11-10).
- No plant identification from a photo: a track the person stopped (brand-book → Do and don't → Stopped tracks,
  2026-11-11).
