## Look

- **What the picture is made of:** one drawn windowsill per film (a plant in its pot, the window, the light on the
  sill), flat shapes with soft shading; the phone appears only in `product` films. No footage, no photographs.
  Source: `notes-style.md` §2 (read 2026-11-10); the human (2026-11-11).
- **Illustrative first, here:** the picture shows the thing the line names. Soil is drawn as soil that dries and
  cracks, light as a beam with dust in it, water as one drop that lands, a reminder as the phone's lock screen.
  Source: `notes-style.md` §2, frames 1–4 (read 2026-11-10).
- **Pace:** a beat holds until its line is read, about 1.5 to 2.5 s; one travelling camera, no cuts, pushing in on
  what each line names and pulling out to the whole sill. Source: the human, "slower" (2026-11-11).
- **Text on screen:** 120 to 132 px, inside the safe area, one line of at most six words at once, on a soft shade.
  Source: `notes-style.md` §2 (read 2026-11-10).

## Language

- **Address:** "you" and "your plants"; a plant by its common name ("fern", "snake plant"), never its Latin one; the
  day is a "water day". Source: `README.md:25` (read 2026-11-09).
- **Tone:** dry and calm, the humour about the plants, never about the person. Source: `README.md:27`.
- **Series:** Windowsill Physics — "If you <do the simple thing>, you've got a <thing>." — "No. <n>", top left from
  frame 0. Source: `marketing/strategy.md` → Rubrics (2026-11-10).
- **Series:** One Number — "One number about your <plant or pot>: <number>." — "#<n>", bottom right from frame 0.
  Source: `marketing/strategy.md` → Rubrics (2026-11-10).
- **Series:** The Turn — "From <date>, your plants <what changes>." — "Turn <n>", top left from frame 0. Source:
  `marketing/strategy.md` → Rubrics (2026-11-10).
- **The end:** the payoff or the first picture again; never a call, a logo or a link. Here the plant is back where
  frame 0 found it, a few days drier, so the loop is the story. Source: the human (2026-11-11).
- **Caption grammar:** line 1 is the hook as it reads on screen; one or two plain sentences that add a fact the film
  had no room for; a credit line for outside material as the last line of words; then three hashtags, the plant's
  name first. Source:
  `notes-style.md` §2 (read 2026-11-10).

## Devices

Two or more things that repeat in every post, so the account is recognisable with its logo covered.

- The same windowsill: the window on the left, the low sun from the right, a small cactus at the edge of the sill, in
  every film. Source: the human (2026-11-11).
- A count of days since the last water, in the top right corner, in `muted`, rolling as the film's days pass. Source:
  `notes-style.md` §2, frame 4 (read 2026-11-10).
- The drop: every film has one drop of water that lands, in `accent`, with a ring from where it lands. Source: the
  human, "keep the drop" (2026-11-11).

## Sound and voice

- **Voice:** voice-free: text on screen and the on-screen seam. Source: the human (2026-11-09).
- **Music:** none. A source's song is never the bed. Source: the human (2026-11-09).
- **Sound effects:** a hit on every visible action, from the kit in `films/sfx/`; soft ticks for the days, one
  `drop` for the water. Source: `notes-style.md` §2 (read 2026-11-10).

## Not this

- No pastel "plant mom" look, no hand-lettered fonts, no stock photographs of plants: the niche's most worn habits.
  Source: `notes-style.md` §1 (read 2026-11-10).
- No plant with a face and a voice: the human ruled it out ("the plants don't talk", 2026-11-11).
