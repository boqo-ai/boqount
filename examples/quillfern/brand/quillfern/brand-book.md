## Legal

- **AI label.** Films are drawn, not realistic, and voice-free: no label is needed. A voiced film would carry the
  platform's AI label, set by the person on each channel. Source: the person, 2026-11-09.
- **Music.** None for now: the films use the synthesized sound kit only. The one exception: a source's own sound under a shot of
  that same source, for that shot's length, when the human's word for that source names its sound (Do and don't →
  Outside material). A source's song is never a film's bed.
- **Other people.** Another person's face, name or channel only with their written permission; never leaked or
  pre-release material, whatever anyone says.
- **Partnerships.** None now; any would be disclosed in the platform's own tool. Source: the person, 2026-11-09.
- **Engagement.** No bought or swapped engagement. Source: `marketing/strategy.md` → What not to do.

## CTA

No organic post asks for anything: no line, picture or caption asks for a comment, a code word, a follow, a click or
a visit, and no post ends on a logo or a link. The call lives on the profile. Setting it is the human's step: the
agent drafts the words and says what to pin.

- **The bio:** per channel, what the brand does and then the call line for an ad post (below), inside the platform's
  bio limit (Instagram 150 characters, TikTok 80, read on their help pages 2026-11-11).
  Instagram: "Water reminders for every plant you own. One message on the right day. Add your first plant at
  quillfern.example." TikTok: "Water reminders for every plant. Add your first plant at quillfern.example." Source:
  `README.md:14`, as drafted at onboarding and set by the person on 2026-11-11.
- **The link:** https://quillfern.example/start on both channels, no tag (`accounts.md` → Links).
- **Pinned posts:** none yet. Once it exists, first the product film that shows the reminder at work.
- **The brand's own pinned comment:** under each product film, "Quillfern sends one reminder on the day a plant needs
  water. The link is in the bio." Source: drafted at onboarding, approved by the person 2026-11-11.
- **The call line for an ad post:** "Add your first plant at quillfern.example." The bio ends on it; of the posts,
  only one the human will promote with bought views carries it, on their word for that post (the record's `Ad: yes
  (<their words, date>)`), and it carries exactly this line. No code word.

### Outside material

Material that is not the brand's goes into a post only on the human's word for that source, given after the terms of
whoever owns it and the risk were put to them once, in plain words. By default a shot is picture only. Its sound goes in only
when their word names it, and only after they were told once that a source's music often belongs to a third owner
and that platforms match sound automatically, so a post can be muted or blocked. Another person's face, name or
channel without their written permission, and leaked or pre-release material, stay out whatever the word.

A word for a source does not cover ad use. When a post becomes an ad (`<skill>/check.md` item 3), each source in it is
put to the human again for that use, with what the terms of whoever holds its rights say about advertising; without that word the ad cut
carries none of it.
