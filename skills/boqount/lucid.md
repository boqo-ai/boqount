# Lucid: the dream flight

## Overview

A generated look (`gen.md`) for a film that holds a wide audience by withholding the full value until the end, through
images people only see in dreams: one camera flight with no cuts, 3D, every product mechanism shown as one impossible
image. It suits a `product` or `offer` film whose mechanism is abstract (reach, chances, copies, time) and a `niche`
film about a feeling. The locked format (`film.md` → The locked format) holds unchanged; the pipeline is `gen.md` →
The pipeline, with what this file adds.

## The rules of the look

1. **Every product mechanism is one dream image**, named in the line under it. Never eye candy without a job. The
   film's image bank (in `films/<slug>/gen/look.md`) maps each mechanism the script names to one image, the way
   these do: a single post is a door of gold light standing in a black mirror sea; strangers are a river of firefly
   lights; followers are a floating town whose lit windows are people; many tries are thirty suns; copies are a prism,
   one beam in and thirty out; the product is the whole world shrinking into one tile of its own screen.
2. **One impossibility per image:** an everyday thing at an impossible scale, in an impossible place, or with
   impossible physics. People stay anonymous (from behind, or hands only).
3. **One flight, no cuts.** Transitions are dream logic: through a surface, a scale jump, a portal, or a pull-back that
   reveals the world inside an object.
4. **The time of day carries the emotion:** blue hour into night for the problem, dawn into sunrise for the value,
   back to blue hour on the seam. One colour means the viewer's own thing and the winner; the brand's accent appears
   only inside the product's interface.
5. **The hero object is steady.** When many things fail and one survives, they fail in one clean wave; the survivor
   is the most prominent object and never flickers or changes identity; nothing flies off at random; and the camera
   goes through the survivor into the next scene (the last lit door becomes the phone's screen).
6. **Text over the dream:** the brand's display face, white, sentence case, on a wider dark halo than usual (the
   plates go white-bright); the frame-0 title in two tones with the payoff phrase in the warm colour, its second tone
   checked for contrast on the brightest sky; key words in the accent box, which grows with the word and is never
   visible empty.

## Delayed value

The retention timeline of this look, free to use in any film, drawn or generated:

- frame 0 is the whole promise, and the most impossible picture;
- by 2–3 s, a pattern the viewer knows (a trend, a meme's shape) or a twist;
- a punchline by about 8 s;
- a reversal by about a quarter of the length;
- then one answered piece of the promise per line, each bigger than the last;
- the film's one burst at about 55–60 %;
- the product named at about 85 %, where a pull-back shows the whole world inside it;
- the seam replays the hook, which now reads as true.

A meme carried into a lucid film keeps its setup and loses its confession line: the flip is the camera flying through
the last lit thing, not the meme's punchline.

## The film's steps

1. The look bible and the image bank, then the look test: four key frames, two takes each (`gen.md` → The pipeline),
   and the person's yes.
2. Key frames, each an edit of the one before, two takes each; every state the voice describes is in its key frame.
3. Segments between keys (start and end frames), checked for cuts; a big jump gets an in-between key.
4. The plate: joins morphed and the joining frame appended, 120 fps, anchored to the cues by the take's own events.
5. Layers in code: the hook drawn twice for the picture loop, the captions, the product's real interface with camera
   push-ins so its labels read, one glow sweep only.
6. Sound: foley per visible action (`gen.md` → The pipeline, step 7); a censor beep, when a line needs one, laid
   exactly over the word, found by its share of energy above 4 kHz; the product's name spliced from an approved take
   when a voice keeps mangling it (`film.md` → The voice).
7. The feed file and its checks (`film.md`).

**What it costs, as an order of size:** a 45–50 s film took about 40 key frames and 100 s of video. Price both at
estimate time (`gen.mjs price`) and quote them before the look test.
