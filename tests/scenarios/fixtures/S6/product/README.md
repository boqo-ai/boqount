# Quillfern

Quillfern is a plant-care app for iPhone and Android. You add your plants once; it learns each one's watering rhythm
from the species, the pot size and the room's light, and reminds you on the right day. Mark a plant as watered and
its schedule adjusts.

- Free: up to 5 plants.
- Quillfern Plus: unlimited plants, a light meter that uses the phone camera, and care notes. $3.99 a month or $29 a year.
- Made by two people in Lisbon. First release: March 2026.
